Grenade Game

[Genre][Platform][Status]

Grenade game is a top down rougelite where you wake up, stranded in a small town with no memory of why you're there, or how you got there. Your only hint is the crashed plane that you woke up in, and the hords of zombies advancing on you. You find some guns in the crashed plane, but none of them work, and you must survive with only a few grenades, trying to find some more weapons. As you explore the area, encounter and document zombies, and find artifacts and tools, you start to remember what happened, who you are, and why you are here.

About

A small team, shipping real games

Maximum Dirt Studios is an independent game studio. We're a small team that prefers finishing things over talking about them — the goal on every project is a compact, polished game you can actually play, not a pitch deck.

We work close to the metal: small teams, tight scope, and enough craft that a five-minute demo still feels considered. If you want to know more about a specific project or what we're building next, use the form above.
